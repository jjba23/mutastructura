USAGE: mutastructura [OPTIONS]
OPTIONS:
    -h, --help
        Display this help message and exit.
    -d, --database
        The engine you are targeting (~sqlite3~, ~postgresql~, ~mysql~).
    -c, --connection
        The connection string or path to your database.
    -m, --migrations
        The path to your ~*.sql~ migration files.
    -r, --resources
        (optional) Path to internal application resources (used for development and installation)
        bundled from ./resources dir
